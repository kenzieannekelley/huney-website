What Huney is

Not a chore chart. Not a blank task app. A home operating layer.

Most household tools make you build the system yourself. Huney starts with the shape of your home, then helps create, schedule, and share the work that normally lives in one person's head.

The point

Make the work visible without making anyone feel managed.

Huney is meant to feel warm, practical, and human. It gives the household a plan without turning home into a performance dashboard.

Why it exists

For the moment when the list in your head gets too heavy.

Home can be full of care, routines, errands, maintenance, and tiny obligations, but still lack one shared place where all of that work can live.

Huney helps the household see what needs attention, who can take it, and when it fits into the week.

Less carrying the household plan alone.

Fewer tense check-ins about what got done.

Less forgetting when maintenance last happened.

Frequently asked questions

What to know about Huney

A quick guide to the household management app and the home planning problems it is built to solve.

What is Huney?

Huney is a shared household management app. It turns chores, meals, calendars, reminders, maintenance, and weekly goals into one practical plan that the people in a home can see and manage together.

Who is Huney for?

Huney is designed for couples, families, roommates, and busy adults who want a clearer way to coordinate the work of running a home.

What can a household manage in Huney?

A household can use Huney to manage recurring tasks, ownership, reminders, meals, recipes, shared calendars, pets, car care, maintenance history, and weekly goals.

How is Huney different from a basic chore app?

A basic chore app usually starts with a blank list. Huney starts with the shape of the home and helps create a realistic household baseline, then supports assignments, rotations, reminders, meals, calendars, and maintenance in the same shared system.

Does Huney support shared households?

Yes. Huney supports shared households so members can see assigned and open tasks, coordinate plans, and keep household information in sync.

What is included in Regular Huney?

Regular Huney includes personalized home setup, shared tasks and reminders, Apple Calendar planning, meal plans and grocery lists, weekly goals, kid completion approvals, car care, Vacation Mode, up to 50 manually entered inventory items, and up to 10 appliance records.

What does Huney Premium add?

Premium adds multiple households and properties, Receipt Memory imports, unlimited inventory and appliance records, weather-aware seasonal preparation, calendar-aware travel prep, room reset plans, rental turnover, and emergency preparation.
